A legacy of faith and education

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. has served learners in Kabankalan City since 1927. KCC is committed to quality and inclusive Catholic education rooted in the Gospel.

  • Academic excellence and lifelong learning
  • Faith formation and Catholic values
  • Community engagement and meaningful service
  • Holistic development of every learner
