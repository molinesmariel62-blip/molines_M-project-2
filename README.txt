KABANKALAN CATHOLIC COLLEGE WEBSITE PROJECT
===========================================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract KCC_Kabankalan_Catholic_College_Website.zip.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in a browser.
4. Keep the assets folder beside the HTML files. Do not move the HTML pages out of the folder.

PAGES
- index.html       Home
- about.html       About KCC
- courses.html     Course/program overview
- trainers.html    Publicly identified school administrators
- events.html      Publicly documented KCC activities
- pricing.html     Sample fee estimates
- contact.html     Campus address, contact details, and map

NAVIGATION
The navigation links keep the requested separate HTML destinations:
index.html, about.html, courses.html, trainers.html, events.html, pricing.html, contact.html.

IMPORTANT CONTENT NOTES
- Tuition figures are illustrative sample estimates for this student website project, not official KCC tuition rates. The BSIT example is shown as PHP 21,000 per semester as requested. Other program amounts are also estimates. Confirm all actual tuition, miscellaneous, and laboratory fees with KCC.
- Course names are based on public KCC-related listings and materials. Program availability may change. Confirm the current official list with the Registrar.
- Leadership names and roles are taken from a publicly available 2025 KCC benchmarking announcement and may have changed. Confirm current appointments with KCC.
- Event cards describe publicly documented examples, not a promise of upcoming event dates.
- KCC-specific photos are linked from publicly accessible image sources. An internet connection is needed to load those images. All other template assets remain included locally.
