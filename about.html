About the Institution

Kabankalan Catholic College

Catholic Education: Our Identity, Our Mission

Since 1927

A legacy of faith and learning

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. (KCC) is a Catholic educational institution located in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. Founded in 1927, the College has a long history of serving learners and the wider community.

KCC describes itself as a Christ-centered learning community committed to academic excellence, faith formation, and service.

Motto: “Teach Ye All Nations” — Matthew 28:19

What We Stand For

The Four Pillars

Academic Excellence

Encouraging critical thinking, competence, and a love for learning.

Faith Formation

Deepening faith through Catholic tradition, prayer, and Gospel values.

Community Service

Putting faith into action through meaningful community engagement.

Holistic Development

Forming knowledgeable, responsible, and values-oriented individuals.

Our Mission

Education that transforms

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.
